# Operating cadence — what you actually do

Sellman is built so your involvement is a yes/no queue, a weekly read, and a monthly decision. No calls.

## Daily (≈ 5 minutes, on your phone)
- `node src/index.js approvals` (or the digest email) — approve/reject community replies, content, first sends
- Glance at the kill-switch log if anything paused

## Weekly (≈ 20 minutes, Monday)
- Read `reports/weekly-YYYY-MM-DD.md` written by the strategist: attributed revenue, funnel by channel,
  experiments, what the brain changed, and ≤ 3 proposed bets with kill criteria
- Approve or reject each bet

## Monthly (≈ 45 minutes)
- Gate check against `ROADMAP.md` — is the current phase's gate met? If the time-box passed, pick a pivot
- Pricing review (strategist proposes, you decide)
- Cost vs. attributed revenue

## Things that always require your approval
- Publishing anything new customer-facing (pages, listings, posts)
- Price or guarantee changes
- First send of any outbound campaign or lifecycle template
- ICP changes
- Any action in a new jurisdiction or on a new platform

## Things that never require approval
- Research, scoring, drafting, attribution, reports
- Pausing (the system can always stop itself; only you can start risky things)
