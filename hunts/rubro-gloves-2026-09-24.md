# Rubro gloves: posted-requirement hunt (2026-09-24)

## Prompt

(household, industrial, examination, surgical), in India (Kerala/Kottayam first) or for export.
Sources: GeM, CPPP, Kerala e-tenders, KMSCL, hospital/PSU tenders, UNGM, B2B RFQs
(IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Go4WorldBusiness, Tradekey, Tradewheel), import data (Volza, Seair, ImportKey).
For each: buyer, quantity, deadline, contact route, cost. No generic directories.
