# Bodies whose rules make buyers buy gloves (and how Rubro fits)

## 1. Tea
- Tea Board / trustea Plant Protection Code (PPC) - latest v19, April 2026. The safe-use guideline every estate follows for spraying. [verified: teaboard.gov.in]
- trustea code - worker safety incl. PPE maintenance; audited by approved bodies (INDOCERT, Cotecna, GCL), every alternate year after year 1. [verified]
- Rainforest Alliance Farming Annex - PPE as prescribed by pesticide label/MSDS. [verified]
- UPASI Tea Research Foundation, Meppadi - advises all Wayanad estates. [verified]
- Fit: pesticide guidance calls for unlined, elbow-length, chemical-resistant gloves -> Rubro 16"-18" All Purpose.
- Adapt: get a chemical-resistance test report (EN ISO 374) on the 16"/18"; give UPASI and auditors a one-page spec.

## 2. Rubber plantations (Kottayam - home ground)
- Rubber Board HQ Kottayam; Rubber Producers' Societies buy inputs for smallholders. Spraying needs gloves. [confirm Board advisory wording]
- Adapt: pitch to RPS as a natural-rubber product made from members' own rubber.

## 3. Haritha Karma Sena
- Suchitwa Mission, Kudumbashree, Clean Kerala Company (state company working with HKS). Kit: coat, helmet, shoes, gloves, raincoat; tenders list "kitchen gloves" / "rubber gloves", sometimes panchayat name printed. [verified]
- Adapt: an "HKS kit" SKU - 14" heavy glove, panchayat name printing offered.

## 4. Standards gap = opportunity
- No BIS standard for general rubber work gloves: IS 4148 is surgical, IS 6994-1 is leather/cotton, IS 8807 is only a selection guide. [verified]
- So estates, seafood exporters and importers ask for European tests: EN 388 (mechanical), EN ISO 374 (chemical).
- One lab report on EN 388 + EN ISO 374 qualifies Rubro for estates, seafood exporters and export RFQs at once.

## 5. Seafood processors
- Export plants are audited (EIC/HACCP, EU buyers); gloves must be food-contact safe. [confirm]
- Adapt: food-contact declaration for the household/all-purpose line.
