# How a new seller actually gets a Wayanad glove order

Verified = found in a public source. Confirm = likely, check on the ground.

## The buyer map (Wayanad)
- 26 local bodies: 23 grama panchayats + 3 municipalities (Kalpetta, Mananthavady, Sulthan Bathery). [verified: wayanad.gov.in]
- Each has a Haritha Karma Sena unit. HKS units are registered micro enterprises under Kudumbashree, paid from household user fees. [verified: Equator Initiative 2024]

## Who signs the order
- Grama panchayat: the Village Extension Officer (VEO) implements the HKS kit project.
  Thirunelli's tender ref was VEO/TENDER/01/2022-23, "Boot, Glouse, Uniform and other items to HKS", closing 26 Dec 2022. [verified]
- Municipality: usually the health section (Health Inspector). [confirm]

## The rule that decides who gets the order
- Below Rs 20,000: quotations by "local inquiry". No advert. The VEO phones 3 suppliers he knows. [verified: Stores Purchase Manual]
- Rs 20,000 and above: open tender. Above Rs 5 lakh: e-tender. [verified]
- Kerala Public Procurement Policy (GOs 2020-2022): price and purchase preference for Kerala MSMEs; Dec 2023 amendment on performance-security exemption for Udyam MSMEs. [verified: spd.kerala.gov.in 10_2023]
  -> Rubro (Kottayam manufacturer, Udyam) qualifies; the traders who usually win do not manufacture.

## What a new seller does, in order
1. Get onto the VEO's quotation list in each panchayat. Visit, leave: 14" and 16" samples, printed rate list (incl. GST), Udyam copy, one line: "Kerala MSME manufacturer - eligible for State procurement preference."
2. Meet the HKS unit secretary at the panchayat MCF (Material Collection Facility). Gloves wear out monthly; ask who buys replacements and from which money. [confirm]
3. Kalpetta, one trip: Kudumbashree District Mission, Suchitwa Mission district office, Haritha Keralam Mission district coordinator. They hold all 26 HKS unit contacts and run district meetings where every secretary attends - ask for 5 minutes there.
4. The tender is a bundle (boots + gloves + uniform). Either:
   a. supply gloves to the trader who wins the bundle, or
   b. bid the full kit with a Kudumbashree tailoring unit (uniform) + a boot dealer. [confirm that LSGs favour Kudumbashree units for apparel]
5. Timing: Thirunelli bought in December. Visit Oct-Dec (pending plan purchases before 31 March) and again Apr-May (new plan projects).

## Second Wayanad lane
Tea/coffee estates (Harrisons Malayalam has Wayanad estates, 15,000+ workforce company-wide). Estate office / manager buys PPE for spraying and field work. [confirm gloves are bought locally vs Kochi HQ]
