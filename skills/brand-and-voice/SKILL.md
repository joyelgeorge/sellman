# Brand and voice

Taskman's brain answers "is this true and provable." This skill answers the question Taskman's brain
was never built to ask: "does this sound like us, and does anyone feel anything reading it?" Load it
alongside claims-and-compliance, never instead of it — voice never overrides a guard violation.

## The voice, until a brand-strategist run says otherwise
- Plain, specific, a little dry. Numbers over adjectives. "₹47,000 across 12 disputed charges" beats
  "significant recovered revenue."
- One operator talking to one buyer, not "our team" or "we're excited to announce."
- Confident about what's proven, openly uncertain about what isn't ("we haven't verified this at your
  scale yet" is voice-correct, not a weakness to hide).
- No filler enthusiasm, no exclamation points, no "game-changing," "revolutionary," "seamless."

## What this skill changes about a draft
- Cut the first sentence if it's a throat-clear ("In today's fast-paced world of SaaS billing…")
- Replace abstract nouns with the concrete thing: not "leakage," but "the ₹ that left through failed
  retries, refunds, and disputes."
- One idea per sentence where the topic is money — a buyer skims for the number, not the prose.
- Read it aloud. If it sounds like it was written to sound smart rather than to be understood, cut it.

## Consistency across surfaces
An audit email, a GEO page, and a community reply are one voice at three lengths, not three voices.
If `brand_voice` context is present in the input (the latest approved voice guide from `brand-strategist`),
it overrides the defaults above — that guide is what buyers have actually seen and reacted to, this
skill's defaults are only the starting point before one exists.

## What this skill does not do
It never invents a claim, softens a guard rejection, or adds urgency language that outbound/claims skills
would reject. Voice is how true things are said, never a reason to say untrue or ungated things.
