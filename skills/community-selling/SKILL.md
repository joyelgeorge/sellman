# Community selling

The rule: **be the most useful answer in the thread.** Selling is a side effect.

## Find
- Search terms come from voice-of-customer phrases, not product words ("customers' cards keep failing",
  "stripe retries not working", "UPI autopay mandate failed", "stock doesn't match tally").
- Respect each community's rules on self-promotion, automation, and links. Store the rule summary per community.
- Only use official APIs or allowed access; respect robots.txt and rate limits.

## Qualify a thread
Reply only if: the question is recent · we can answer substantively without mentioning the product · the
community allows the kind of reply we'd write.

## Draft
1. Answer the question fully first — steps, numbers, caveats. Useful even if they never click.
2. If relevant, one line: "Disclosure: I build [face], which does this automatically — free audit here if useful."
3. No link at all when the community forbids it or when the answer stands better without it.

## Approval
Every community post goes to the approval queue. No auto-posting. Posts are made by the operator's real,
disclosed account — not sock puppets, not AI personas.

## Learn
Log the question, the phrasing, and whether it led to an audit. Questions that recur become GEO pages.
