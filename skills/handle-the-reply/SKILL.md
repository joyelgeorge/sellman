# handle-the-reply

Trigger: someone answers. This is where Taskman has historically stalled.

## Rule

Answer with proof, price, and a single next step. Do not pitch a second feature. Do not ask them to create a Taskman account.

## Output

Reply draft + stage move to replied or killed.

## Kill

Ghosting. Pitching Taskman-the-platform. Asking them to sign up.
