# Partner channel

Partners already have the relationships and trust. Sellman's job is to make referring effortless and
paying partners automatic.

## Partner types by wedge
- Revenue recovery: Stripe/Razorpay implementation consultants, SaaS agencies, fractional CFOs
- Tally shrinkage: authorised Tally partners, CA firms, retail POS installers
- Renegotiation: fractional CFOs, bookkeeping firms, startup accelerators

## Self-serve partner kit
1. Partner page: who it's for, what their clients get, what partners earn, terms
2. Signup → instant referral link + co-branded audit link
3. Dashboard: referred audits, installs, verified fees, payouts
4. Payouts computed from Taskman verified fees (same source of truth as North Star)
5. Asset pack: one-page explainer, sample audit report (demo data), approved copy blocks

## Terms to define
revenue share % and duration · attribution window · payout threshold and schedule · prohibited practices
(no spam, no misleading claims, disclosure of referral relationship)

## Recruiting without calls
- List the partner program on the same pages partners already visit (integration pages, marketplace listing)
- Community posts in partner communities (with approval)
- Personal, evidence-led email to partners who publicly advise on the pain (subject to outbound rules)

## Rules
- Partners get the same claims guard: shared copy blocks only
- Written agreement before any Tally partner listing or co-branded material
