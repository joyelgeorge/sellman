# verify-buyer-before-contact

Trigger when a scouted name is about to become a message.

## Rule

Re-derive the finding from public evidence. Count real issues, not dashboard leftovers. No contact if the pain cannot be shown.

## Output

Verified finding count, evidence snippet, go / no-go.

## Kill

Contacting on a stale critical flag. Taskman already watched 19 flagged leads collapse to 4 with real secrets.
